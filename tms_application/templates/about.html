Pioneering Modern Freight Logistics

Transport Management System (TMS) bridges the gap between commercial consignors and professional fleet drivers across India. Our technology-driven platform automates consignment dispatching, manages verified fleets across 6 vehicle classifications, and ensures seamless cargo transit with end-to-end transparency.

  • Automated Vehicle & Driver Matching

    Intelligent dispatch engine automatically matches consignments to verified on-duty drivers based on cargo capacity and truck specifications.

  • Verified Fleet & Equipment Standards

    Every commercial driver is verified with verified driver licenses (DL), vehicle RC registration, and standardized payload classifications.

  • End-to-End Milestone Tracking

    Zero-login 5-stage milestone tracking enables customers and recipients to monitor consignments from processing to doorstep delivery.
